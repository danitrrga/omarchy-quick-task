Quick Task

Press a key, type a sentence, press Enter. Date, project, section, label and priority are read out of it.

  • English and Spanish dates, live. tomorrow 9am, lunes a las 9
  • Your own projects, sections and labels autocomplete.
  • A CLI for your terminal and your agents.
terminal · or any agent
