## Design Context

### Users

Daniel uses Quick Task from anywhere in Omarchy to get a thought into Todoist before it interrupts the work in front of him. The primary interaction is keyboard-first, lasts a few seconds, and must work equally well in English and Spanish.

### Brand Personality

Quiet, exact, and immediate. It should feel like an instrument built into Omarchy rather than a separate branded app: restrained enough to disappear, confident enough to make recognition state obvious.

### Aesthetic Direction

Native Omarchy utility with a disciplined industrial edge. Fonts, colors, spacing, and corner geometry come from the live Omarchy shell theme. The memorable element is the recognition line: the sentence stays human while parsed details become precise, reversible signals beneath it. No copied Todoist chrome, floating glass, fixed palette, or fixed radius.

### Design Principles

- Open focused, accept text immediately, and submit with Enter.
- Reveal structure only after it is recognized; never turn quick capture into a form.
- Inherit live Omarchy font, color, spacing, and corner radius tokens on every surface.
- Keep parsing reversible: recognized date text can be restored as literal task text.
- Preserve the user’s text on every error and explain the next action plainly.
